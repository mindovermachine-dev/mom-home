## Dont miss a beat - stay updated with MoM!

We have set up a handful of open google groups, they serve different purposes and you can join one or more of them to stay updated on different aspects of MoM's activities.

👇[If your email address is not a Google account](#wrap-email)👇

### Office-hours

Every Tuesday at 8\:15 AM in the morning we join a video call on Matrix and discuss the world, politics, current affairs, this and that, ongoing projects, upcoming events, and any other relevant topics related to MoM. It is open to anyone interested in participating. The mail group `office-hours@mindovermachine.dk` receives calendar invites. So if you join the group, the events will appear in your calendar automatically. You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Updates

The `updates@mindovermachine.dk` group is used to keep everyone informed about new events, essays, briefs, software releases, member news, and other MoM-related updates. By joining this group, you will receive email notifications whenever new content is published. The mail group `updates@mindovermachine.dk` receives these notifications, so if you join the group, you will get instant updates in your inbox. You can unsubscribe at any time.
