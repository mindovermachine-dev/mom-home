## Dont miss a beat - stay updated with MoM!

We have set up a open google group <a href="https://groups.google.com/a/mindovermachine.dk/g/updates" target="_blank">`updates@mindovermachine.dk`</a>. The group receives an email when new stuff is published. Join the group and you will get instant updates in your inbox on new events, essays, briefs, software releases, member news and other MoM stuff.

You can adjust your group settings to receive each update, a daily digest or you can silence it, so you don't get any emails, but just occasionally check in on the group yourself. You can unsubscribe at any time.
