We help teams make architecture decisions that improve adaptability over time, while still supporting near-term delivery pressure.

## Focus areas

- Architecture decision facilitation
- Bounded context and dependency mapping
- Runtime and operational concerns in design choices
- Socio-technical alignment between teams and systems

## Outputs you can use

- Decision records with explicit trade-offs
- Clear transition paths from current to target architecture
- Guardrails for future changes

## Good fit when

- Decision debt is slowing execution
- Team boundaries and system boundaries conflict
- You need to modernize without a risky full rewrite
