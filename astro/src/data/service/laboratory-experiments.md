Laboratory engagements are short, focused pilots in your real environment. They are designed to reduce uncertainty before larger organizational moves.

## Experiment design

- Clear hypothesis and expected effect
- Baseline signals before the change
- Explicit constraints and stop criteria
- Lightweight retrospective and recommendation

## Typical pilot scopes

- New branching and release model in one product stream
- AI-supported coding workflow with quality gates
- Alternative planning and estimation practice

## Good fit when

- You want evidence before broad transformation
- Previous change programs felt too big and too risky
- You need to prove value with limited effort
