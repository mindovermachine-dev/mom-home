We work with teams in their daily delivery system, not in abstract frameworks. The goal is to reduce friction while increasing confidence in change.

## Focus areas

- Trunk-based development and release readiness
- Shift-left quality with automated feedback loops
- Collaborative AI involvement in everyday delivery
- Feature slicing and incremental rollout patterns

## Engagement style

- Hands-on pairing with delivery teams
- Working sessions with engineering leadership
- Small experiments with short feedback cycles

## Good fit when

- Lead time is rising while quality is dropping
- Code review and testing feel like bottlenecks
- AI usage is increasing but standards are unclear
