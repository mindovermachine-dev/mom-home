We map your end-to-end delivery flow and identify where value gets delayed, lost, or degraded.

## What we analyze

- Work intake and prioritization
- Handoffs and waiting states
- Build, test, and release pipeline behavior
- Feedback loops from production and users

## Signals we look for

- Muda: unnecessary work and rework
- Mura: unevenness and batching effects
- Muri: overload and unsustainable pressure

## Good fit when

- Delivery feels busy but outcomes are inconsistent
- Teams struggle to explain where work slows down
- You need a practical baseline for improvement
