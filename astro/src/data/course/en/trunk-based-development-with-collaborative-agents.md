This hands-on workshop treats trunk-based development as an end-to-end way of working: changes stay small, integration happens often, feedback comes quickly and main remains ready to release. We also explore how AI agents can contribute without blurring responsibility for quality or decisions.

## Trunk-based practice

- Small tasks, short-lived branches and frequent integration to trunk.
- A clean, linear and releasable main, with each atomic change traceable to a specific piece of work.
- Automated checks, tests and builds as fast feedback, bringing quality assurance close to the work instead of saving it for a late gate.
- Tests focused on observable behavior rather than implementation details, making change and refactoring easier.
- Issue-centred work logs that keep decisions, questions and progress with the task.

## Working with agents

- Give agents a clear specification, acceptance criteria and relevant repository instructions.
- Make the repository a predictable workspace with containers, stable test data and checks that can also run locally.
- Let agents work on short-lived branches and propose changes; people remain accountable for decisions, review and integration to main.
- Use test and check results to give concrete feedback to both the agent and the team. When collaboration exposes ambiguity, improve the specification, repository instructions or guardrails.
- Choose review practices that fit your workflow: unnecessary hand-offs and manual approval gates can add waiting time, while focused review and clear ownership still matter.

## What you will take away

Try a non-blocking workflow on a real task and identify what your specifications, repository, tests and team practices need in order to integrate frequently without compromising quality or accountability.
