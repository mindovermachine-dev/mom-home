Get to know the components of the MoM FOSS Dev Stack and how they can support software development without unnecessary dependence on proprietary SaaS platforms.

## What we explore

- Forgejo for source hosting, CI/CD and issue tracking.
- Matrix for team and community communication.
- Locally run open-source language models for AI-assisted development.
- Practical considerations for operating, securing and adapting the stack.

## What you will take away

A practical basis for assessing how a FOSS-based development stack could work in your organization and what it would take to run a pilot.
