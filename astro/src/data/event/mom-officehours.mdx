## This is MoM's weekly Office Hours meeting

The meeting is run as a Matrix [video call](https://matrix.to/#/#mom-office-hours:matrix.org) in our room [`#mom-office-hours:matrix.org`](https://matrix.to/#/#mom-office-hours:matrix.org) and is open to anyone who wants to join.

This meeting is an integrated part of our _Community of Practice_. It's open to _everyone_; We advocate full transparency and _Legitimate Peripheral Participation_. In short: no matter what your interest or incentive is — You are so welcome 🤩.

At this meeting we discuss everything under the sun: the week that was, tech news, and the tasks ahead of us. The agenda is set by whoever shows up.

**You should join if:**

- You have an idea you want to share or get feedback on
- You are already in one of the MoM circles and want to share your progress or get feedback
- You want to understand what regenerative software is or why it matters
- You are just curious and don't want anything in particular — just curious...
- You are new here and would like a tour of our community and to get to know us
