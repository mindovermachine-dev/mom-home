## This is MoM's weekly Tech Talk meeting

The meeting is run as a [video call](https://app.slack.com/huddle/T09DW7D2W4Q/C0B4SUYJW4R) in in our Matrix room [`#mom-office-hours`](https://matrix.to/#/#mom-tech-talks:matrix.org) and is open to anyone who wants to join.

This meeting is an integrated part of our _Community of Practice_. It's open to _everyone;_ We advocate full transparency and _Legitimate Peripheral Participation_. In short, no matter what you interest or incentive is — You are so welcome 🤩.

**You should join if:**

- You are working on one of our FOSS projects and want to share your progress or get feedback
- You would like to _join_ one of our FOSS projects and want to be onboarded
- You see a fantastic potential or perspective in one of our projects and would like to discuss it with the team

### Lean Coffee style

The meeting is are run in a [Lean Coffee](https://leancoffee.org/) style. This means that we will start with a blank agenda and the participants will propose topics to discuss. The topics will be voted on and the most popular ones will be discussed first. Each topic will have a timebox of 8 minutes and we will use a timer to keep track of time. If a topic is not finished within the timebox, we will vote on whether to continue or move on to the next topic.

### Dont miss a beat - get the Office Hours invites in your calendar!

We have set up a open google group [`office-hours@mindovermachine.dk`](https://office-hours.mindovermachine.dk) which is invited to all office hour meetings. If you join the group, you will see the invites along with all your other calendar events. You can ignore or reject the invites if you don't want to attend a specific meeting.
