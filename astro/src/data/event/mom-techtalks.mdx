## This is MoM's weekly Tech Talk meeting

The meeting is run as a matrix [video call](https://matrix.to/#/#mom-tech-talks:matrix.org) in in our room [`#mom-tech-talks:matrix.org`](https://matrix.to/#/#mom-tech-talks:matrix.org) it is open to anyone who wants to join.

This meeting is an integrated part of our _Community of Practice_. It's open to _everyone;_ We advocate full transparency and _Legitimate Peripheral Participation_. In short, no matter what you interest or incentive is — You are so welcome 🤩.

On this meeting we discuss technical content related to [our projects](/projects). The agenda is set by those present at the meeting.

**You should join if:**

- You are working on one of our FOSS projects and want to share your progress or get feedback
- You would like to _join_ one of our FOSS projects and want to be onboarded
- You see a fantastic potential or perspective in one of our projects and would like to discuss it with the team
