The EU's Cyber Resilience Act introduces an important role: the open source software (OSS) steward. The role has sometimes been misunderstood, fuelling speculation that open source will become harder to use in commercial products.

At this event, we'll look beyond the myths and explore the problem the steward role aims to address, why it was formalised, and what it means in practice. We'll also discuss what your business or organisation can do if you use open source in your products or services, or have suppliers develop open source on your behalf, and are unsure whether you comply with the CRA. Even if you already know you don't, the discussion is for you.

There will be plenty of time for questions and discussion.
