Erik Rosenberg is a project leader and learning consultant with a background in both teaching and IT. He holds a cand.IT in IT, learning and organisational change from Aalborg University and is a trained teacher. He is a certified PRINCE2 Agile Practitioner.

In recent years, Erik has worked with technology and AI in organisations and has repeatedly seen the same pattern: the tool is rarely the hard part. The harder challenge is building the foundations beneath it, such as shared understanding, trust, and the ability to intervene when something goes wrong. He is developing a hybrid human/AI workflow for knowledge work, drawing on the UNIX philosophy, Engelbart, Obsidian, and Second Brain, and grounded in a desire for digital sovereignty.
